# Evening Star Pattern — Complete Bearish Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Evening Star Pattern is one of the strongest multi-candlestick bearish reversal patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It signals:
- Buyer exhaustion
- Market indecision
- Strong bearish reversal potential

The pattern forms after:
- A bullish move
- Aggressive buying
- Momentum exhaustion

The Evening Star Pattern is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the highest probability bearish reversal candlestick formations.

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

The Evening Star consists of three candles:

1. Strong bullish candle
2. Small indecision candle
3. Strong bearish candle
