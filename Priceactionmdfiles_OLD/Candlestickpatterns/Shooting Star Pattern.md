# Shooting Star Pattern — Complete Bearish Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Shooting Star Pattern is one of the most important bearish reversal candlestick patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It signals:
- Buyer exhaustion
- Strong rejection from higher prices
- Potential bearish reversal

The Shooting Star is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the strongest single-candle bearish reversal patterns.

---

# 2. Structure of the Shooting Star Pattern

The Shooting Star contains:

1. Small real body
2. Long upper wick
3. Small or no lower wick

---

# Visual Structure

                High
                  |
                  |
                  |
                  |
                  |
                []
                []
                []
                []
            Open/Close

---

# Key Characteristics

- Upper wick at least 2x body size
- Small candle body
- Candle closes near bottom
- Very small lower shadow

Body color:
- Red preferred
- Green still valid

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Shooting Star represents:
# Strong rejection of higher prices

---

# What Happens Internally

1. Buyers push market aggressively upward
2. Breakout buying occurs
3. Institutions absorb buying pressure
4. Sellers step in aggressively
5. Price collapses before candle closes

This creates:
# Long upper wick

Meaning:
- Buyers failed to maintain control
- Sellers regained strength

---

# 4. Why the Shooting Star Works

The pattern works because:
- It traps late buyers
- Reveals strong supply zone
- Signals institutional selling

The long upper wick shows:
# Liquidity sweep + rejection

This is extremely important in:
- Smart money trading
- Liquidity concepts
- Institutional price action

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Shooting Star

# A. Prior Uptrend Required

Most important rule.

Shooting Star must form after:
- Uptrend

- Pattern loses reliability

# B. Long Upper Wick

Upper wick should:
- Be at least 2x body size

Stronger rejection

# C. Small Real Body

Small body indicates:
- Weak bullish closing pressure

# D. Minimal Lower Wick

# E. Weak Close

Best Shooting Stars close:
- Near candle lows

Represents:
# Seller dominance
