# Neutral Candlestick Price Action Patterns — Complete Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

Neutral Candlestick Patterns are price action structures that represent:
- Market indecision
- Temporary balance between buyers and sellers
- Volatility compression
- Potential reversal OR continuation

Unlike bullish or bearish patterns:
# Neutral patterns do NOT directly predict direction.

Instead, they signal:
- Uncertainty
- Energy buildup
- Liquidity accumulation
- Possible upcoming expansion move

Neutral candlestick patterns are widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

Professional traders use them to:
- Detect consolidation
- Identify traps
- Prepare for breakouts
- Understand liquidity behavior

---

# 2. Core Philosophy of Neutral Candles

Neutral candles represent:
# Temporary equilibrium in market control

Neither:
- Buyers dominate fully
NOR
- Sellers dominate fully

Instead:
- Market pauses
- Liquidity builds
- Volatility compresses

This often occurs before:
# Major directional movement

# 4. Doji Pattern

# Structure

Open and close nearly equal.

Body:
- Extremely small

Wicks:
- Can vary

---

# Visual Structure

          |
          |
      ----+----
          |
          |

---

# Psychology

Represents:
# Complete indecision

Buyers and sellers both:
- Attempt control
- Fail to dominate

---

# Important Meaning

Doji indicates:
- Momentum slowing
- Possible reversal
OR
- Consolidation continuation

---

# Best Usage

Near:
- Support
- Resistance
- Trend exhaustion
- Breakout zones

# 5. Long-Legged Doji

# Structure

Very long upper and lower wicks.
Tiny body.

---

# Psychology

Represents:
# Extreme volatility + indecision

Market:
- Moves aggressively both directions
- Fails to hold either side

---

# Meaning

- Strong battle between bulls and bears
- Uncertainty increasing
- Breakout approaching

---

# 6. Dragonfly Doji

# Structure

Tiny body near top.
Long lower wick.

---

# Psychology

Sellers push strongly downward.

BUT:
- Buyers completely recover price

Represents:
# Bullish rejection

---

# Important

Technically neutral,
BUT:
- Bullish bias exists at support

---

# 7. Gravestone Doji
