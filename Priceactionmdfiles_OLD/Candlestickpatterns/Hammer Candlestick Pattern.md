# Hammer Candlestick Pattern — Complete Bullish Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Hammer Candlestick Pattern is one of the most important bullish reversal patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It signals:
- Seller exhaustion
- Strong rejection from lower prices
- Potential bullish reversal

The Hammer is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the strongest single-candle reversal patterns.

---

# 2. Structure of the Hammer Pattern

The Hammer contains:

1. Small real body
2. Long lower wick
3. Small or no upper wick

---

# Visual Structure

          Close/Open
              []
              []
              []
              []
              |
              |
              |
              |
              |
              |
              Low

---

# Key Characteristics

- Lower wick at least 2x body size
- Small candle body
- Candle closes near top
- Very small upper shadow

Body color:
- Green preferred
- Red still valid

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Hammer represents:
# Strong rejection of lower prices

---

# What Happens Internally

1. Sellers push market aggressively downward
2. Panic selling occurs
3. Buyers absorb selling pressure
4. Institutions step in
5. Price recovers strongly before candle closes

This creates:
# Long lower wick

Meaning:
- Sellers failed to maintain control
- Buyers regained strength

---

# 4. Why the Hammer Works

The Hammer works because:
- It traps late sellers
- Reveals strong demand zone
- Signals institutional buying

The long lower wick shows:
# Liquidity sweep + rejection

This is critical in:
- Smart money trading
- Liquidity concepts
- Institutional price action

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Hammer

# A. Prior Downtrend Required

Most important rule.

Hammer must form after:
- Downtrend

- Pullback

- Pattern loses reliability

# B. Long Lower Wick

Lower wick should:
- Be at least 2x body size

Stronger rejection

---

# C. Small Real Body

Small body indicates:
- Weak closing pressure from sellers

---

# D. Minimal Upper Wick

Better bullish strength

# E. Strong Close

- Near candle highs

# Buyer dominance
