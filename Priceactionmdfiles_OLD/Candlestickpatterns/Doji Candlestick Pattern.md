# Doji Candlestick Pattern — Complete Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Doji Candlestick Pattern is one of the most important neutral candlestick patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It signals:
- Market indecision
- Balance between buyers and sellers
- Momentum slowdown
- Potential reversal OR continuation

The Doji forms when:
- Open price and close price are nearly equal

The Doji is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the most important market psychology candles.

---

# 2. Structure of the Doji Pattern

The Doji contains:

1. Extremely small body
2. Upper wick
3. Lower wick

---

# Visual Structure

          High
            |
            |
      ------+------
            |
            |
          Low

---

# Key Characteristics

- Open ≈ Close
- Very tiny body
- Wicks can vary in size
- Represents equilibrium

---

# Important Rule

The smaller the body:
# The stronger the indecision

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Doji represents:
# Complete balance between buyers and sellers

---

# What Happens Internally

1. Buyers push market upward
2. Sellers push market downward
3. Neither side gains full control
4. Price closes near opening price

This creates:
# Neutral equilibrium candle

---

# Meaning

The market is:
- Uncertain
- Pausing
- Deciding next direction

Often:
# Large moves follow Doji candles

---
