# Bearish Engulfing Pattern — Complete Bearish Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Bearish Engulfing Pattern is one of the strongest bearish reversal candlestick patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It signals:
- Seller dominance
- Buyer exhaustion
- Strong bearish momentum shift

The pattern forms when:
- A bearish candle completely engulfs the previous bullish candle body

The Bearish Engulfing Pattern is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the highest probability bearish reversal candlestick patterns.

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

The pattern contains two candles:

1. Bullish candle
2. Strong bearish engulfing candle

---

     |      |

     |______|

  ______________
 |              |

# Key Characteristics

- First candle bullish
- Second candle bearish
- Second candle body larger
- Bearish body fully engulfs prior body

Best patterns:
- Have strong bearish close
- Show momentum expansion

# 3. Core Psychology

The Bearish Engulfing Pattern represents:
# Complete shift in market control

---

# What Happens Internally

## First Candle

Buyers dominate market.

Bullish sentiment continues.

---

## Second Candle

Initially:
- Buyers continue pushing higher

BUT:
- Sellers aggressively absorb buying
- Strong bearish selling enters
- Price reverses sharply downward

Eventually:
- Sellers overpower buyers completely

This creates:
# Full engulfing bearish candle

---

# Meaning

- Buyer control failed
- Seller aggression increased
- Momentum shifted bearish
