# Spinning Top Candlestick Pattern — Complete Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Spinning Top Candlestick Pattern is one of the most important neutral candlestick patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It signals:
- Market indecision
- Momentum slowdown
- Temporary equilibrium between buyers and sellers
- Potential reversal OR continuation

The Spinning Top forms when:
- The candle has a small real body
- Both upper and lower wicks are visible

The Spinning Top is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the most important market hesitation candles.

---

3. Lower wick

# 3. Core Psychology

The Spinning Top represents:
# Temporary balance between buyers and sellers

---

# What Happens Internally

1. Buyers push price upward
2. Sellers push price downward
3. Neither side maintains control
4. Price closes near opening level

This creates:
# Small-bodied indecision candle

---

# Meaning

The market is:
- Losing momentum
- Pausing
- Re-evaluating direction

Often:
# Important moves follow Spinning Tops
