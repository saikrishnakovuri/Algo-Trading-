# Morning Star Pattern — Complete Bullish Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Morning Star Pattern is one of the strongest multi-candlestick bullish reversal patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It signals:
- Seller exhaustion
- Market indecision
- Strong bullish reversal potential

The pattern forms after:
- A bearish move
- Panic selling
- Momentum exhaustion

The Morning Star Pattern is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the highest probability bullish reversal candlestick formations.

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

The Morning Star consists of three candles:

1. Strong bearish candle
2. Small indecision candle
3. Strong bullish candle
