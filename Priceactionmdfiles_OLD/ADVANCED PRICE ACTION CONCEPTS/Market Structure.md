- Bullish control
- Bearish control
- Consolidation
- Trend continuation
- Trend reversal

# 3. Basic Components of Market Structure

Market structure contains:

1. Higher Highs (HH)
2. Higher Lows (HL)
3. Lower Highs (LH)
4. Lower Lows (LL)

---

# Bullish Structure

HH → HL → HH → HL

Represents:
# Buyer dominance

---

# Bearish Structure

LL → LH → LL → LH

Represents:
# Seller dominance

---

# 4. Higher High (HH)

# Definition

Price creates:
- New high above previous high

Represents:
# Bullish strength

---

# Psychology

Buyers:
- Aggressively push market upward

Institutions:
- Continue accumulation

---

# 5. Higher Low (HL)

# Definition

Price creates:
- Pullback low above previous low

Represents:
# Strong bullish continuation

---

# Psychology

Buyers defend:
- Higher prices

Represents:
# Market confidence

---

# 6. Lower Low (LL)

# Definition

Price creates:
- New low below previous low

Represents:
# Bearish strength

---

# Psychology

Sellers:
- Aggressively push market downward

Institutions:
- Continue distribution

---

# 7. Lower High (LH)

# Definition

Price creates:
- Rally high below previous high

Represents:
# Weak bullish recovery

---

# Psychology

Buyers:
- Losing strength

Sellers:
- Maintain control
