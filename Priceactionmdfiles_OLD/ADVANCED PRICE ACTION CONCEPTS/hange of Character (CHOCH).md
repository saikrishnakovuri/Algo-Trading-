# 2. Definition of CHOCH

# Change of Character (CHOCH)

CHOCH occurs when:
# Price breaks a key structural point against the current trend direction

This indicates:
# Possible trend reversal

---

# Bullish CHOCH

During bearish trend:
- Lower High (LH) breaks upward

Represents:
# Possible bullish reversal

---

# Bearish CHOCH

During bullish trend:
- Higher Low (HL) breaks downward

Represents:
# Possible bearish reversal

---

# Core Logic

Trend weakens →
Structure breaks →
Momentum shifts →
Possible reversal begins
