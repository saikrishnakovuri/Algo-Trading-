# 2. Definition of Supply & Demand

# Demand Zone

A demand zone is:
# An area where buying pressure exceeds selling pressure

Price tends to:
# Move upward from this area

---

# Supply Zone

A supply zone is:
# An area where selling pressure exceeds buying pressure

Price tends to:
# Move downward from this area

---

# Core Logic

Demand > Supply →
Price rises

Supply > Demand →
Price falls
