# 2. Definition of LH & LL

# Lower High (LH)

Price creates:
# A rally high below previous high

Represents:
- Weak bullish recovery
- Seller control

---

# Lower Low (LL)

Price creates:
# A new low below previous low

Represents:
- Strong bearish momentum
- Seller dominance

---

# Core Structure

LL → LH → LL → LH

Represents:
# Healthy bearish trend
