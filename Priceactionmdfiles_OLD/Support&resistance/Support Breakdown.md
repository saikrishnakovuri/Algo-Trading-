# Support Breakdown — Complete Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

A Support Breakdown is one of the most powerful bearish concepts in:
- Technical analysis
- Price action trading
- Momentum trading
- Institutional trading
- Smart money concepts

A support breakdown occurs when:
# Price breaks strongly below a support zone

This signals:
- Sellers overpowering buyers
- Bearish momentum expansion
- Liquidity release
- Potential trend continuation or reversal

Support breakdowns are widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

Professional traders use support breakdowns to:
- Enter bearish trends
- Catch momentum moves
- Identify institutional distribution
- Detect volatility expansion
- Trade bearish continuation

---

# 2. Definition of Support Breakdown

# Support Breakdown

A support breakdown occurs when:
- Price moves below support
AND
- Sellers maintain control below the level

---

# Basic Idea

Price compresses above support →
Selling pressure builds →
Buyers weaken →
Price collapses downward
