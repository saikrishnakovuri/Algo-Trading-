# 1. Introduction

Resistance is one of the most important concepts in:
- Technical analysis
- Price action trading
- Institutional trading
- Smart money concepts
- Market structure analysis

Resistance represents:
# A price zone where selling pressure becomes stronger than buying pressure

At resistance:
- Sellers step in
- Buyers weaken
- Price often reacts downward

Resistance is used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

Professional traders use resistance to:
- Find reversals
- Enter short trades
- Place stop losses
- Identify liquidity zones
- Detect institutional distribution

# 2. Definition of Resistance

# Resistance

A price level or zone where:
- Supply exceeds demand
- Sellers absorb buying pressure
- Price tends to stop rising

---

# Basic Idea

When price approaches resistance:
- Sellers become aggressive
- Institutions distribute positions
- Buyers lose momentum

This often causes:
# Price rejection downward
