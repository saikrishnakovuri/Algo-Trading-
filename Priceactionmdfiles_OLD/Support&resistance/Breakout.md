# Breakout — Complete Price Action Trading Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

A Breakout is one of the most important concepts in:
- Technical analysis
- Price action trading
- Momentum trading
- Institutional trading
- Smart money concepts

A breakout occurs when:
# Price moves strongly beyond a key level or structure

These levels include:
- Resistance
- Support
- Trendlines
- Channels
- Triangles
- Ranges
- Consolidation zones

Breakouts are widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

Professional traders use breakouts to:
- Catch momentum moves
- Enter trends early
- Identify institutional activity
- Detect volatility expansion
- Trade liquidity shifts

---

# 2. Definition of Breakout

# Breakout

A breakout occurs when:
- Price escapes a defined structure
AND
- Momentum expands strongly

---

# Simple Idea

Price compresses →
Energy builds →
Liquidity accumulates →
Explosive move begins
