# 1. Introduction

Support is one of the most important concepts in:
- Technical analysis
- Price action trading
- Institutional trading
- Smart money concepts
- Market structure analysis

Support represents:
# A price zone where buying pressure becomes stronger than selling pressure

At support:
- Buyers step in
- Sellers weaken
- Price often reacts upward

Support is used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

Professional traders use support to:
- Find reversals
- Enter trades
- Place stop losses
- Identify liquidity zones
- Detect institutional activity

# 2. Definition of Support

# Support

A price level or zone where:
- Demand exceeds supply
- Buyers absorb selling pressure
- Price tends to stop falling

---

# Basic Idea

When price approaches support:
- Buyers become interested
- Institutions accumulate
- Sellers lose momentum

This often causes:
# Price bounce upward
