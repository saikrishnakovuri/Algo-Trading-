# 1. Introduction

A Resistance Breakout is one of the most powerful bullish concepts in:
- Technical analysis
- Price action trading
- Momentum trading
- Institutional trading
- Smart money concepts

A resistance breakout occurs when:
# Price breaks strongly above a resistance zone

This signals:
- Buyers overpowering sellers
- Momentum expansion
- Liquidity release
- Potential trend continuation or reversal

Resistance breakouts are widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

Professional traders use resistance breakouts to:
- Enter bullish trends
- Catch momentum moves
- Identify institutional accumulation
- Detect volatility expansion
- Trade breakout continuation

# 2. Definition of Resistance Breakout

# Resistance Breakout

A resistance breakout occurs when:
- Price moves above resistance
AND
- Buyers maintain control above the level

---

# Basic Idea

Price compresses below resistance →
Buying pressure builds →
Sellers weaken →
Price explodes upward
