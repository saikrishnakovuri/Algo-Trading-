# 1. Introduction

The Retest Entry is one of the most powerful and professional entry techniques in:
- Price action trading
- Smart money trading
- Institutional trading
- Breakout trading
- Trend continuation trading

A retest entry occurs when:
# Price breaks an important level and then revisits it before continuing in the breakout direction

This concept is widely used by:
- Professional traders
- Institutions
- Hedge funds
- Algorithmic traders

Retest entries are used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

Professional traders prefer retest entries because they provide:
- Better accuracy
- Lower risk
- Better RR ratio
- Confirmation of breakout strength

# 2. Definition of Retest Entry

# Retest Entry

A retest entry occurs when:
1. Price breaks a key level
2. Price revisits the broken level
3. The level holds
4. Price continues in breakout direction

---

# Simple Logic

Breakout →
Retest →
Confirmation →
Continuation
