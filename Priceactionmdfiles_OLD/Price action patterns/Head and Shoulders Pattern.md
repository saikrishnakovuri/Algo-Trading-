# Head and Shoulders Pattern — Complete Price Action Strategy Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Head and Shoulders Pattern is one of the most powerful and reliable reversal patterns in technical analysis.

It signals:
- Trend exhaustion
- Momentum weakening
- Possible market reversal

The pattern is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the strongest reversal structures in price action trading.

---

# 2. Types of Head and Shoulders

There are two major variations:

# A. Standard Head and Shoulders

Bearish reversal pattern.

Forms after:
- Uptrend

Signals:
- Potential bearish reversal

---

# B. Inverse Head and Shoulders

Bullish reversal pattern.

Forms after:
- Downtrend

Signals:
- Potential bullish reversal

---

# 3. Structure of the Pattern

# Standard Head and Shoulders Structure

The pattern contains:

1. Left Shoulder
2. Head
3. Right Shoulder
4. Neckline

---

# Visual Structure

        Head
          /\
         /  \
        /    \
 Left  /      \ Right
Shoulder       Shoulder
     /\         /\
    /  \       /  \

-------------------------
        Neckline

Eventually:
- Neckline breaks
- Reversal occurs

---

# 4. Core Psychology

The Head and Shoulders pattern represents:
- Buyer exhaustion
- Momentum failure
- Shift in market control

# Pattern Psychology

## Left Shoulder

Buyers still strong.

---

## Head

Buyers push price to new highs.

BUT:
- Momentum weakens internally

---

## Right Shoulder

Buyers fail to create higher high.

This is critical.

It signals:
# Bullish weakness

Eventually:
- Sellers take control
- Neckline breaks
- Reversal accelerates

---

# 5. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Trend participants become trapped
- Smart money distributes positions
- Momentum deteriorates gradually

The neckline becomes:
# Critical support level

When neckline breaks:
- Stop losses trigger
- Panic exits occur
- Trend reverses

---

# 6. Conditions for a Valid Head and Shoulders

# A. Prior Trend Required

Standard H&S:
- Requires uptrend first

Inverse H&S:
- Requires downtrend first

Without trend:
- Pattern loses reliability

---

# B. Clear Shoulders

Shoulders should:
- Be visually identifiable
- Have similar height/structure

Perfect symmetry is NOT required.

---

# C. Head Must Be Highest Point

For standard pattern:
- Head higher than shoulders

For inverse:
- Head lower than shoulders

---

# D. Valid Neckline

Neckline connects:
- Swing lows (standard)
OR
- Swing highs (inverse)

Can be:
- Horizontal
- Sloping

---

# E. Volume Behavior

Ideal behavior:
- High volume during left shoulder
- Reduced volume during head
- Weak volume during right shoulder
- Volume spike during neckline break

---

# 7. Timeframes

Works on:
- 5m
- 15m
- 1H
- 4H
- Daily
- Weekly

Most reliable:
- 1H+
- Daily+

Lower timeframe:
- More fakeouts
- More noise

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Neckline Breakout Entry

# Bearish Entry (Standard Pattern)

Enter when:
- Candle closes below neckline

Requirements:
- Strong bearish candle
- Volume expansion

---

# Bullish Entry (Inverse Pattern)

Enter when:
- Candle closes above neckline

Requirements:
- Strong bullish candle
- Volume spike

---

# Stop Loss

Standard:
- Above right shoulder

Inverse:
- Below right shoulder

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique:

Target =
Distance from head to neckline projected from breakout point

---

# Example

Head:
22,300

Neckline:
22,000

Difference:
300 points

Breakdown:
22,000

Target:
21,700

---

# Strategy 2 — Retest Entry (Professional Method)

Most professional traders prefer:
- Breakout
- Retest
- Confirmation

---

# Standard Pattern

1. Neckline breaks
2. Price retests neckline
3. Neckline becomes resistance
4. Bearish rejection occurs

Enter after confirmation.

---

# Inverse Pattern

1. Neckline breaks upward
2. Price retests neckline
3. Neckline becomes support
4. Bullish confirmation occurs

---

# Benefits

- Better RR
- Lower fake breakout probability
- Higher confirmation quality

---

# 9. Advanced Entry Confirmation

# A. Volume Expansion

Breakout volume should:
- Exceed average volume
- Show aggressive participation

---

# B. RSI Divergence

Very powerful confirmation.

Higher high

RSI:
Lower high

Signals:
# Weakening momentum

Common before reversal.

---

# C. VWAP Confirmation

Bearish:
- Price below VWAP after breakdown

Bullish:
- Price above VWAP after breakout

---

# D. EMA Alignment

Bearish:
20 EMA < 50 EMA

Bullish:
20 EMA > 50 EMA

---

# 10. Fake Breakouts

Very common in:
- Options expiry
- Crypto
- News volatility

---

# Bull Trap

Price:
1. Breaks neckline upward
2. Attracts buyers
3. Reverses sharply

---

# Bear Trap

Price:
1. Breaks neckline downward
2. Attracts sellers
3. Reverses upward

---

# Signs of Fake Breakout

## Weak volume

## Long breakout wick

## Immediate reversal

## Reclaim of neckline

---

# Avoiding Fakeouts

Use:
- Candle close confirmation
- Retest entries
- Volume confirmation
- HTF alignment

---

# 11. Institutional Concepts

# A. Distribution Phase

Standard H&S often represents:
# Institutional Distribution

Institutions:
- Sell gradually near highs
- Reduce positions quietly
- Trap late buyers

---

# B. Accumulation Phase

Inverse H&S often represents:
# Institutional Accumulation

Institutions:
- Buy during weakness
- Build positions quietly

Eventually:
- Bullish breakout occurs

---

# C. Liquidity Engineering

Head often sweeps:
- Previous highs
- Liquidity pools
- Retail breakout traders

Then:
- Sharp reversal begins
