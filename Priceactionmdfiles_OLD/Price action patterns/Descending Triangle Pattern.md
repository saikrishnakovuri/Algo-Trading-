# Descending Triangle Pattern — Complete Price Action Strategy Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Descending Triangle is one of the strongest bearish continuation patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It forms when:
- Price creates LOWER HIGHS
- Price repeatedly tests the SAME SUPPORT LEVEL

This creates:
- Flat horizontal support
- Falling resistance trendline

The structure represents:
- Increasing seller aggression
- Buyer exhaustion
- Pressure buildup above support

Eventually:
- Support breaks
- Strong bearish expansion occurs

The pattern is widely used in:
- Intraday trading
- Swing trading
- Futures trading
- Options trading
- Crypto trading
- Index trading (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Descending Triangle represents:
- Sellers becoming increasingly aggressive
- Buyers defending support repeatedly
- Demand weakening over time

# Important Observation

Every bounce becomes weaker.

This means:
- Sellers enter earlier
- Buyers lose momentum
- Support absorbs repeated selling pressure

Eventually:
- Buyers become exhausted
- Support collapses

This is called:
# Demand Exhaustion

One of the most important bearish institutional concepts.

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Liquidity accumulates below support
- Retail traders place stop losses below support
- Institutions slowly build short positions

As price compresses:
- Volatility decreases
- Energy builds

Breakdown often causes:
- Panic selling
- Long liquidation
- Momentum acceleration

---

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Descending Triangle

# A. Flat Support

Support should be:
- Clearly visible
- Tested multiple times

Minimum:
- 2 touches

Better:
- 3 or more touches

---

# B. Lower Highs

Price should create:
- Lower highs consistently

This confirms:
- Seller aggression

---

# C. Volume Contraction

During formation:
- Volume should gradually decrease

Meaning:
- Consolidation
- Reduced participation

---

# D. Breakdown Volume Expansion

During breakdown:
- Volume should increase sharply

Without volume:
- Higher fake breakdown probability

---

# E. Breakdown Before Apex

Best breakdowns occur around:
- 50% to 75% of pattern completion

Weak patterns:
- Reach apex fully without move

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Breakdown Entry

# Bearish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes below support

Requirements:
- Strong bearish candle
- Increased volume
- Momentum confirmation

---

# Entry Confirmation

Ideal breakdown candle:
- Large body
- Small lower wick
- Strong close near lows

Avoid:
- Weak breakdown candles
- Long lower wicks

---

# Stop Loss

Place above:
- Last lower high
OR
- Descending resistance trendline

---

# Target

Use:
Measured Move Technique

Target =
Height of triangle projected downward from breakdown point

# 10. Fake Breakdowns

Very common in:
- Options
- Crypto
- Illiquid stocks

---

# Bear Trap Structure

Price:
1. Breaks support
2. Attracts sellers
3. Reverses sharply upward
4. Moves back above support

This traps breakout sellers.

---

# Signs of Fake Breakdown

## Weak volume

## Long lower wick

## Immediate reversal

## Re-entry into triangle

---

# Avoiding Fakeouts

Use:
- Candle close confirmation
- Retest entries
- Volume analysis
- Higher timeframe trend alignment
