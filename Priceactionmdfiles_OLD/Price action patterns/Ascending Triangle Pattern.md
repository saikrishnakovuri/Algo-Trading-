# Ascending Triangle Pattern — Complete Price Action Strategy Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Ascending Triangle is one of the strongest bullish continuation patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It forms when:
- Price creates HIGHER LOWS
- Price repeatedly rejects from the SAME RESISTANCE LEVEL

This creates:
- Flat resistance
- Rising support

The structure represents:
- Increasing buyer aggression
- Seller exhaustion
- Pressure buildup beneath resistance

Eventually:
- Resistance breaks
- Strong bullish expansion occurs

The pattern is commonly used in:
- Intraday trading
- Swing trading
- Options trading
- Crypto trading
- Futures trading
- Index trading (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

# 3. Core Psychology

The Ascending Triangle represents:
- Buyers becoming increasingly aggressive
- Sellers defending a key resistance
- Demand increasing over time

# Important Observation

Every dip becomes smaller.

This means:
- Buyers are entering earlier
- Sellers are losing control
- Supply near resistance gets absorbed

Eventually:
- Supply becomes insufficient
- Breakout occurs

This is called:
# Supply Absorption

One of the most important institutional concepts.

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Liquidity accumulates above resistance
- Retail traders place stop losses below higher lows
- Institutions slowly accumulate positions

As compression increases:
- Volatility decreases
- Energy builds

Breakout often causes:
- Short covering
- FOMO buying
- Momentum expansion

---

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Ascending Triangle

# A. Flat Resistance

Resistance should be:
- Clearly visible
- Tested multiple times

Minimum:
- 2 touches

Better:
- 3 or more touches

---

# B. Rising Support

Price should create:
- Higher lows

This confirms:
- Buyer aggression

---

# C. Volume Contraction

During formation:
- Volume should decrease gradually

Meaning:
- Consolidation phase
- Reduced participation

---

# D. Breakout Volume Expansion

During breakout:
- Volume should spike strongly

Without volume:
- Higher fake breakout probability

---

# E. Breakout Before Apex

Best breakouts occur around:
- 50% to 75% of pattern completion

Weak patterns:
- Reach full apex without breakout

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Breakout Entry

# Bullish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes above resistance

Requirements:
- Strong bullish candle
- Increased volume
- Momentum confirmation

---

# Entry Confirmation

Ideal breakout candle:
- Large body
- Small upper wick
- Strong close near highs

Avoid:
- Weak breakout candles
- Long upper wicks

---

# Stop Loss

Place below:
- Last higher low
OR
- Rising support trendline

---

# Target

Use:
Measured Move Technique

Target =
Height of triangle added to breakout point

---

# Example

Resistance:
22,000

Support:
21,900

Height:
100 points

Breakout:
22,000

Target:
22,100

Best professional entry method.

4. Bullish confirmation candle appears

Enter after confirmation.

# 10. Fake Breakouts

Very common in:
- Options
- Crypto
- Low liquidity stocks

---

# Bull Trap Structure

Price:
1. Breaks resistance
2. Attracts breakout buyers
3. Reverses sharply
4. Falls back below resistance

This traps retail traders.

---

# Signs of Fake Breakout

## Weak volume

## Long upper wick

## Immediate rejection

## Breakdown below breakout candle

---

# Avoiding Fakeouts

Use:
- Candle close confirmation
- Retest entries
- Volume analysis
- Higher timeframe alignment

# 11. Institutional Concepts

- Buy stop orders

# B. Supply Absorption
