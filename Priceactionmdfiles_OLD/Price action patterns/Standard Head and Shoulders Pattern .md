# Standard Head and Shoulders Pattern — Complete Bearish Reversal Price Action Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Standard Head and Shoulders Pattern is one of the most reliable bearish reversal patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It forms after:
- A strong bullish trend
- Momentum exhaustion
- Institutional distribution

The pattern signals:
- Buyer weakness
- Trend reversal probability
- Shift from bullish to bearish market control

It is considered:
# One of the strongest bearish reversal patterns in trading.

The pattern is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

The pattern contains four major components:

1. Left Shoulder
2. Head
3. Right Shoulder
4. Neckline

---

# Visual Structure

             Head
               /\
              /  \
             /    \
 Left       /      \ Right
Shoulder   /        \ Shoulder
          /\        /\
         /  \      /  \

--------------------------------
            Neckline

Eventually:
- Price breaks neckline downward
- Bearish reversal begins

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Standard Head and Shoulders represents:
- Bullish exhaustion
- Momentum deterioration
- Institutional selling pressure increasing

---

# Left Shoulder

Buyers still dominate market.

Strong bullish continuation exists.

---

# Head

Buyers push market to new highs.

BUT:
- Momentum weakens internally
- Smart money begins distribution

Retail traders still bullish.

---

# Right Shoulder

Buyers fail to create another higher high.

This is critical.

It signals:
# Bullish weakness

Institutions continue selling.

Eventually:
- Sellers take control
- Neckline breaks
- Bearish reversal accelerates

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Late buyers get trapped
- Institutions distribute near highs
- Momentum weakens progressively

The neckline acts as:
# Major support zone

When support breaks:
- Stop losses trigger
- Panic exits occur
- Short sellers enter
- Downside momentum accelerates

---

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Standard Head and Shoulders

# A. Prior Uptrend Required

The pattern must form after:
- Clear bullish trend

Without prior uptrend:
- Pattern reliability decreases

---

# B. Clear Left and Right Shoulders

Shoulders should:
- Be visually identifiable
- Have relatively similar structure

Perfect symmetry NOT required.

---

# C. Head Must Be Highest Point

Head should:
- Create higher high
- Sweep liquidity above previous highs

This often traps breakout buyers.

---

# D. Valid Neckline

Neckline connects:
- Swing lows between shoulders and head

Can be:
- Horizontal
- Upward sloping
- Downward sloping

---

# E. Volume Behavior

Ideal volume behavior:

## Left Shoulder
Strong bullish volume

## Head
Reduced momentum volume

## Right Shoulder
Weak bullish participation

## Neckline Breakdown
Strong bearish volume expansion

---

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Neckline Breakdown Entry

# Bearish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes below neckline

Requirements:
- Strong bearish candle
- Volume expansion
- Momentum confirmation

---

# Ideal Breakdown Candle

- Large bearish body
- Strong close near lows
- Minimal lower wick

Avoid:
- Weak breakdown candles
- Low volume breakdowns

---

# Stop Loss

Place above:
- Right shoulder high
OR
- Neckline retest zone

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique:

Target =
Distance from head to neckline projected downward

---

# Example

Head:
22,300

Neckline:
22,000

Difference:
300 points

Breakdown:
22,000

Target:
21,700

---

# Strategy 2 — Retest Entry (Professional Method)

Preferred by professional traders.

---

# Steps

1. Neckline breaks downward
2. Price retests neckline
3. Neckline becomes resistance
4. Bearish rejection candle forms

Enter after confirmation.

---

# Benefits

- Better RR
- Lower fake breakdown probability
- Safer entry

Common in:
- Options expiry
- Crypto
- High volatility sessions

---

# Bear Trap Failure

1. Breaks neckline downward
2. Attracts sellers
3. Quickly reverses upward

This traps breakout shorts.

---

# Signs of Fake Breakdown

## Weak volume

## Long lower wick

## Immediate reclaim of neckline

## Strong bullish reversal candle

---

# Avoiding Fakeouts

Use:
- Candle close confirmation
- Retest entries
- Volume confirmation
- HTF trend alignment
