# Double Top Pattern — Complete Bearish Reversal Price Action Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Double Top Pattern is one of the most important bearish reversal patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It forms after:
- A bullish trend
- Strong upward momentum
- Repeated rejection at resistance

The pattern signals:
- Buyer exhaustion
- Resistance strength
- Potential bearish reversal

The Double Top is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the simplest yet most effective reversal patterns.

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

The pattern consists of:

1. First Top
2. Pullback
3. Second Top
4. Neckline
5. Breakdown

---

# Visual Structure

          Top 1        Top 2
            /\          /\
           /  \        /  \
          /    \      /    \
         /      \    /      \
--------/--------\--/--------\-------
              Neckline

Eventually:
- Neckline breaks downward
- Bearish reversal begins

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Double Top represents:
- Failed bullish continuation
- Strong resistance rejection
- Market exhaustion

---

# First Top

Buyers dominate market.

Strong bullish momentum exists.

---

# Pullback

Profit booking begins.

Sellers temporarily gain strength.

---

# Second Top

Buyers attempt another breakout.

BUT:
- Price fails near same resistance
- Momentum weakens
- Buyers lose control

This is critical.

It signals:
# Resistance holding strongly

Eventually:
- Sellers dominate
- Neckline breaks
- Reversal accelerates

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Resistance absorbs buying pressure
- Breakout buyers get trapped
- Institutions distribute positions near highs

The neckline acts as:
# Major support level

When neckline breaks:
- Long liquidation begins
- Stop losses trigger
- Panic selling accelerates

---

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Double Top

# A. Prior Uptrend Required

Pattern must form after:
- Clear bullish trend

Without uptrend:
- Reliability decreases

---

# B. Two Clear Tops

The two highs should:
- Be near similar levels
- Show strong rejection

Perfect equality NOT required.

---

# C. Clear Pullback Between Tops

The pullback:
- Creates neckline support
- Separates the two tops clearly

Weak pullback =
Weak pattern

---

# D. Valid Neckline

Neckline forms at:
- Lowest point between tops

Acts as:
- Major support zone

---

# E. Volume Behavior

Ideal behavior:

## First Top
Strong bullish volume

## Second Top
Lower bullish participation

## Neckline Breakdown
Strong bearish volume expansion

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Neckline Breakdown Entry

# Bearish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes below neckline

Requirements:
- Strong bearish candle
- Volume expansion

---

# Ideal Breakdown Candle

- Large bearish body
- Strong close near lows
- Small lower wick

Avoid:
- Weak breakdown candles
- Low volume breakdowns

---

# Stop Loss

Place above:
- Second top
OR
- Breakdown retest zone

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique:

Target =
Distance from top to neckline projected downward

---

# Example

Top:
22,300

Neckline:
22,000

Difference:
300 points

Breakdown:
22,000

Target:
21,700

---

# Strategy 2 — Retest Entry (Professional Method)

Preferred by professional traders.

---

# Steps

1. Neckline breaks
2. Price retests neckline
3. Neckline becomes resistance
4. Bearish rejection occurs

Enter after confirmation.

---

# Benefits

- Better RR
- Lower fake breakdown probability
- Higher confirmation quality

- Crypto
- Options expiry
- High volatility sessions

## Immediate reversal

## Long lower wick

- Candle close confirmation
