# Symmetrical Triangle Pattern — Complete Price Action Strategy Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Symmetrical Triangle is one of the most powerful and commonly used price action continuation/compression patterns in trading.

It forms when:
- Price creates LOWER HIGHS
- Price creates HIGHER LOWS

This creates a triangular compression zone where volatility decreases until a breakout occurs.

The breakout direction determines the next move.

The pattern works in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY/SPX)

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

## Visual Structure

Resistance Trendline:
- Lower highs

Support Trendline:
- Higher lows

Price gets squeezed between them.

Example:

          /\
         /  \
        /    \
       /      \
------/--------\------
     Price Compression

Eventually:
- Breakout Up
OR
- Breakdown Down

---

# 3. Market Psychology

The pattern represents:
- Buyer and seller equilibrium
- Volatility compression
- Reduced momentum
- Energy buildup

As price compresses:
- Buyers become cautious
- Sellers become cautious
- Stop losses accumulate
- Liquidity builds near breakout zones

Eventually:
- One side gains dominance
- Explosion in volatility occurs

This is why triangle breakouts often produce strong moves.

---

# 4. Conditions for a Valid Symmetrical Triangle

A valid triangle should have:

## A. Minimum Touches

At least:
- 2 lower highs
- 2 higher lows

Better:
- 3+ touches each side

---

## B. Converging Trendlines

Trendlines must move toward each other.

NOT valid:
- Parallel lines
- Expanding structure

---

## C. Volume Contraction

Volume should gradually decrease during formation.

Reason:
- Participation decreases
- Market waits for breakout

---

## D. Breakout Before Apex

Best breakouts happen around:
- 50% to 75% of pattern completion

If price reaches apex fully:
- Breakout strength weakens

---

## B. Bearish Continuation Triangle

# 7. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Aggressive Breakout Entry

## Long Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes above resistance trendline

Conditions:
- Strong bullish candle
- Increased volume

### Stop Loss
Below:
- Last swing low
OR
- Triangle support

### Target
Pattern height projection.

---

## Short Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes below support trendline

Stop Loss:
- Above recent swing high

Target:
- Triangle height

---

# Strategy 2 — Retest Entry (Higher Probability)

Best method.

## Long Retest

Steps:
1. Breakout occurs
2. Price retests broken resistance
3. Resistance becomes support
4. Bullish confirmation candle appears

Enter on:
- Rejection candle
- Engulfing candle
- Strong bullish close

---

## Short Retest

Same logic inverse.

- Breaks downward
