# Cup and Handle Pattern — Complete Bullish Continuation Price Action Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Cup and Handle Pattern is one of the most famous bullish continuation breakout patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It represents:
- Consolidation after bullish trend
- Institutional accumulation
- Momentum rebuilding before breakout

The pattern was popularized by:
- William O'Neil

It is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

The pattern is considered:
# One of the highest probability bullish continuation structures.

---

1. Cup

# 3. Core Psychology

The Cup and Handle represents:
- Recovery from correction
- Gradual return of bullish momentum
- Final consolidation before breakout

---

# Cup Formation

After strong rally:
- Profit booking begins
- Price gradually declines
- Buyers slowly return
- Price recovers back to highs

This forms:
# Rounded accumulation structure

---

# Handle Formation

Near resistance:
- Weak holders exit
- Small pullback occurs
- Institutions absorb supply

This creates:
# Final bullish consolidation

Eventually:
- Resistance breaks
- Momentum accelerates upward

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Institutions accumulate during cup formation
- Weak traders exit during handle
- Liquidity builds above resistance

When breakout occurs:
- Momentum buyers enter
- Short sellers cover
- Institutional buying accelerates

This creates:
# Explosive bullish continuation

---

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Cup and Handle

# A. Prior Bullish Trend Required

The pattern works best after:
- Strong uptrend

Without bullish context:
- Reliability decreases

---

# B. Rounded Cup Structure

Cup should:
- Look smooth and rounded
- Avoid V-shaped sharp recovery

Rounded structure indicates:
- Healthy accumulation

---

# C. Cup Depth

Ideal depth:
- Moderate correction

Too deep:
- Weak structure

Too shallow:
- Weak accumulation

---

# D. Handle Structure

Handle should:
- Drift slightly downward
OR
- Consolidate sideways

Should remain:
- Small relative to cup

---

# E. Volume Behavior

Ideal behavior:

## Left Side of Cup
High selling volume

## Bottom of Cup
Reduced volume

## Right Side Recovery
Increasing bullish volume

## Handle
Volume contraction

## Breakout
Strong volume expansion

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Breakout Entry

# Bullish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes above cup resistance

Requirements:
- Strong bullish candle
- Volume expansion
- Momentum confirmation

---

# Ideal Breakout Candle

- Large bullish body
- Strong close near highs
- Small upper wick

Avoid:
- Weak breakout candles
- Low volume breakouts

---

# Stop Loss

Place below:
- Handle low
OR
- Breakout retest zone

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique:

Target =
Depth of cup projected upward from breakout point

---

# Example

300 points

Breakout:
22,000

22,300
