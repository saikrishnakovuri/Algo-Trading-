# Rectangle / Range Pattern — Complete Price Action Strategy Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Rectangle Pattern, also called a Range Pattern or Box Pattern, is one of the most important price action structures in trading.

It forms when:
- Price repeatedly bounces between a horizontal resistance level
AND
- A horizontal support level

This creates:
- A sideways consolidation zone
- A balance between buyers and sellers
- A liquidity accumulation area

The Rectangle Pattern can be traded in two major ways:

1. Range Trading
2. Breakout Trading

The pattern works in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

# Visual Structure

Resistance
--------------------------------

Price oscillates between levels

--------------------------------
Support

Price repeatedly:
- Rejects resistance
- Bounces from support

Eventually:
- Breakout occurs
OR
- Range continues

# 3. Core Psychology

The Rectangle represents:
- Temporary equilibrium
- Balanced supply and demand
- Market indecision

During the range:
- Buyers defend support
- Sellers defend resistance
- Neither side gains control

Liquidity builds:
- Above resistance
- Below support

Eventually:
- One side dominates
- Expansion phase begins

This creates:
# Volatility Expansion After Compression

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Markets alternate between expansion and consolidation
- Institutions accumulate/distribute positions inside ranges
- Retail traders get trapped near edges

Rectangle patterns create:
- Predictable support/resistance reactions
- Liquidity concentration
- High RR trading opportunities

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Rectangle

# A. Horizontal Resistance

Resistance should:
- Be clearly visible
- Reject price multiple times

Minimum:
- 2 touches

Better:
- 3+ touches

---

# B. Horizontal Support

Support should:
- Hold multiple times
- Show strong reactions

---

# C. Clear Range Boundaries

The range should:
- Be visually obvious
- Avoid chaotic overlapping structure

---

# D. Volume Behavior

Usually:
- Volume decreases inside range
- Volume expands during breakout

---

# E. Multiple Rejections

More touches =
More reliable range

# 8. Trading Approaches

There are TWO major ways to trade rectangles:

# B. Breakout Trading

---

# 9. Range Trading Strategy

# Buy Near Support

Enter when:
- Price approaches support
- Bullish rejection occurs

Confirmation:
- Hammer
- Bullish engulfing
- Strong rejection wick

---

# Stop Loss

Place below:
- Range support

---

# Target

Exit near:
- Resistance

---

# Sell Near Resistance

Enter when:
- Price approaches resistance
- Bearish rejection occurs

Confirmation:
- Shooting star
- Bearish engulfing
- Rejection wick

---

# Stop Loss

Place above:
- Resistance

---

# Target

Exit near:
- Support

---

# 10. Breakout Trading Strategy

# Bullish Breakout

Enter when:
- Candle closes above resistance

Requirements:
- Strong breakout candle
- Volume spike
- Momentum confirmation

---

# Bearish Breakdown

Enter when:
- Candle closes below support

Requirements:
- Strong bearish candle
- Volume expansion

---

# Stop Loss

Bullish:
- Below breakout level

Bearish:
- Above breakdown level

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique:

Target =
Height of rectangle projected from breakout point

---

# Example

Range Height:
100 points

Breakout:
22,000

Target:
22,100

---

# 11. Retest Entry Strategy

Professional traders prefer:
- Breakout
- Retest
- Continuation

---

# Bullish Retest

1. Resistance breaks
2. Price retests resistance
3. Resistance becomes support
4. Bullish candle forms

Enter after confirmation.

---

# Bearish Retest

1. Support breaks
2. Price retests support
3. Support becomes resistance
4. Bearish candle forms

---

# Benefits

- Better RR
- Lower fake breakout probability
- Higher confirmation quality

# 12. Fake Breakouts

Extremely common in:
- Options
- Crypto
- Expiry sessions

---

# Bull Trap

Price:
1. Breaks resistance
2. Attracts buyers
3. Reverses downward sharply

---

# Bear Trap

Price:
1. Breaks support
2. Attracts sellers
3. Reverses upward sharply

---

# Signs of Fake Breakout

## Weak volume

## Long breakout wick

## Immediate reversal

## Re-entry into range

---

# Avoiding Fakeouts

Use:
- Candle close confirmation
- Retest entry
- Volume confirmation
- HTF alignment
