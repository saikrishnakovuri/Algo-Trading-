# Triple Top Pattern — Complete Bearish Reversal Price Action Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Triple Top Pattern is a powerful bearish reversal structure in technical analysis and price action trading.

It forms after:
- A strong bullish trend
- Multiple failed breakout attempts
- Strong resistance rejection

The pattern signals:
- Buyer exhaustion
- Resistance dominance
- High probability bearish reversal

The Triple Top is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# A stronger confirmation reversal pattern than Double Top because resistance holds multiple times.

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

The pattern consists of:

1. First Top
2. Second Top
3. Third Top
4. Neckline
5. Breakdown

---

# Visual Structure

        Top 1     Top 2     Top 3
          /\        /\        /\
         /  \      /  \      /  \
        /    \    /    \    /    \
-------/------\--/------\--/------\-------
                 Neckline

Eventually:
- Neckline breaks downward
- Bearish reversal begins

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Triple Top represents:
- Repeated bullish failure
- Strong institutional resistance
- Gradual seller dominance

---

# First Top

Buyers still control market.

Strong bullish momentum exists.

---

# Second Top

Buyers attempt breakout again.

BUT:
- Resistance holds
- Momentum weakens slightly

---

# Third Top

Final breakout attempt fails.

This is critical.

It signals:
# Buyer exhaustion

Institutions continue distributing.

Eventually:
- Sellers gain full control
- Neckline breaks
- Bearish expansion begins

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Resistance absorbs buying repeatedly
- Breakout traders get trapped multiple times
- Institutions distribute heavily near highs

Each failed breakout:
- Weakens buyers psychologically
- Strengthens sellers emotionally

The neckline becomes:
# Major support zone

When neckline breaks:
- Long liquidation begins
- Stop losses trigger
- Bearish momentum accelerates

---

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Triple Top

# A. Prior Uptrend Required

Pattern must form after:
- Strong bullish trend

Without uptrend:
- Pattern reliability decreases

---

# B. Three Clear Tops

All three highs should:
- Form near same resistance zone
- Show repeated rejection

Perfect equality NOT required.

---

# C. Clear Pullbacks Between Tops

Each pullback should:
- Create identifiable neckline support
- Separate tops clearly

Weak pullbacks =
Weak pattern

---

# D. Valid Neckline

Neckline forms at:
- Lowest swing lows between tops

Acts as:
- Major support zone

---

# E. Volume Behavior

Ideal behavior:

## First Top
Strong bullish volume

## Second Top
Reduced bullish participation

## Third Top
Weak momentum volume

## Neckline Breakdown
Strong bearish volume expansion

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Neckline Breakdown Entry

# Bearish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes below neckline

Requirements:
- Strong bearish candle
- Volume expansion

---

# Ideal Breakdown Candle

- Large bearish body
- Strong close near lows
- Small lower wick

Avoid:
- Weak breakdown candles
- Low volume breakdowns

---

# Stop Loss

Place above:
- Third top
OR
- Neckline retest zone

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique:

Target =
Distance from tops to neckline projected downward

---

# Example

Top:
22,300

Neckline:
22,000

Difference:
300 points

Breakdown:
22,000

Target:
21,700
