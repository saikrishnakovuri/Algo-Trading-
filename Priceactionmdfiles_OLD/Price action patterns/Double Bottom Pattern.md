# Double Bottom Pattern — Complete Bullish Reversal Price Action Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Double Bottom Pattern is one of the most reliable bullish reversal patterns in technical analysis and price action trading.

It forms after:
- A bearish trend
- Heavy selling pressure
- Repeated rejection from support

The pattern signals:
- Seller exhaustion
- Strong support zone
- Potential bullish reversal

The Double Bottom is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

It is considered:
# One of the simplest yet most powerful bullish reversal patterns.

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

The pattern consists of:

1. First Bottom
2. Bounce
3. Second Bottom
4. Neckline
5. Bullish Breakout

---

Eventually:
- Neckline breaks upward
- Bullish reversal begins

# 3. Core Psychology

The Double Bottom represents:
- Failed bearish continuation
- Strong support defense
- Market exhaustion

---

# First Bottom

Sellers dominate market.

Strong bearish momentum exists.

---

# Bounce

Short covering begins.

Buyers temporarily gain strength.

---

# Second Bottom

Sellers attempt another breakdown.

BUT:
- Price fails near same support
- Momentum weakens
- Buyers absorb selling pressure

This is critical.

It signals:
# Strong support holding

Eventually:
- Buyers dominate
- Neckline breaks upward
- Bullish reversal accelerates

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Support absorbs selling pressure
- Breakdown sellers get trapped
- Institutions accumulate positions near lows

The neckline acts as:
# Major breakout resistance

When neckline breaks:
- Short covering begins
- Momentum buyers enter
- Bullish expansion accelerates

---

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Double Bottom

# A. Prior Downtrend Required

Pattern must form after:
- Clear bearish trend

Without downtrend:
- Reliability decreases

---

# B. Two Clear Bottoms

The two lows should:
- Be near similar levels
- Show strong rejection

Perfect equality NOT required.

---

# C. Clear Bounce Between Bottoms

The bounce:
- Creates neckline resistance
- Separates the two bottoms clearly

Weak bounce =
Weak pattern

---

# D. Valid Neckline

Neckline forms at:
- Highest point between bottoms

Acts as:
- Major breakout resistance

---

# E. Volume Behavior

Ideal behavior:

## First Bottom
Strong bearish volume

## Second Bottom
Lower selling participation

## Neckline Breakout
Strong bullish volume expansion

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Neckline Breakout Entry

# Bullish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes above neckline

Requirements:
- Strong bullish candle
- Volume expansion

---

# Ideal Breakout Candle

- Large bullish body
- Strong close near highs
- Small upper wick

Avoid:
- Weak breakout candles
- Low volume breakouts

---

# Stop Loss

Place below:
- Second bottom
OR
- Breakout retest zone

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique:

Target =
Distance from bottom to neckline projected upward

---

# Example

Bottom:
21,700

Neckline:
22,000

Difference:
300 points

Breakout:
22,000

Target:
22,300

---

# Strategy 2 — Retest Entry (Professional Method)

Preferred by professional traders.

---

# Steps

1. Neckline breaks upward
2. Price retests neckline
3. Neckline becomes support
4. Bullish confirmation candle forms

Enter after confirmation.

---

# Benefits

- Better RR
- Lower fake breakout probability
- Higher confirmation quality

# 10. Fake Breakouts

Very common in:
- Crypto
- Options expiry
- High volatility sessions

---

# Bull Trap Failure

Price:
1. Breaks neckline upward
2. Attracts buyers
3. Quickly reverses downward

This traps breakout buyers.

---

# Signs of Fake Breakout

## Weak volume

## Immediate reversal

## Long upper wick

## Neckline failure

---

# Avoiding Fakeouts

Use:
- Candle close confirmation
- Retest entry
- Volume confirmation
- HTF alignment
