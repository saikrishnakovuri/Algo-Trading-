# Falling Wedge Pattern — Complete Price Action Strategy Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Falling Wedge is one of the most powerful bullish price action patterns in technical analysis.

It forms when:
- Price continues moving downward
- Both support and resistance trendlines slope downward
- The range between them gradually narrows

This creates:
- A falling compressed structure
- Weakening bearish momentum
- Increasing probability of bullish reversal

The Falling Wedge is usually:
- A bullish reversal pattern
OR
- A bullish continuation pattern

depending on overall market context.

The pattern is widely used in:
- Intraday trading
- Swing trading
- Options trading
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Index trading (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Falling Wedge represents:
- Sellers still pushing price downward
- But bearish momentum weakening internally

Important observation:
- Each downward move becomes weaker
- Sellers struggle to maintain control
- Buyers slowly absorb supply

This creates:
# Momentum Weakness

Price falls,
but selling strength weakens.

Eventually:
- Sellers exhaust
- Buyers dominate
- Bullish breakout occurs

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Panic sellers continue exiting
- Smart money gradually accumulates positions
- Bearish momentum decreases despite lower prices

This creates:
- Trapped sellers
- Weak bearish continuation
- Increasing upside reversal probability

When breakout occurs:
- Short covering begins
- Buyers enter aggressively
- Momentum accelerates upward

# A. Both Trendlines Must Fall

Support:
Downward sloping

Resistance:
Downward sloping

---

# B. Resistance Must Fall Faster

This creates:
- Narrowing structure
- Compression effect

---

# C. Minimum Touches

- 2 highs
- 2 lows

Better:
- 3+ touches

---

# D. Volume Contraction

During formation:
- Volume should gradually decrease

- Selling participation weakens
- Trend losing strength

---

# E. Breakout Before Apex

Best breakouts occur:
- Before full wedge completion

Usually:
50%–80% into structure

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Breakout Entry

# Bullish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes above wedge resistance

- Strong bullish candle
- Volume expansion
- Momentum confirmation

---

# Entry Confirmation

Ideal breakout candle:
- Large bullish body
- Strong close near highs
- Minimal upper wick

Avoid:
- Weak breakout candles
- Small indecisive candles

---

# Stop Loss

Place below:
- Last swing low
OR
- Wedge support

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique

Height of wedge projected upward
