# Inverse Head and Shoulders Pattern — Complete Price Action Strategy Guide

---

# 1. Introduction

The Inverse Head and Shoulders Pattern is one of the most powerful bullish reversal structures in technical analysis and price action trading.

It forms after:
- A prolonged downtrend
- Heavy selling pressure
- Market exhaustion

The pattern signals:
- Bearish momentum weakening
- Institutional accumulation
- Potential bullish reversal

It is considered:
# One of the strongest bullish reversal patterns in trading.

The pattern is widely used in:
- Stocks
- Forex
- Crypto
- Futures
- Options
- Indices (NIFTY/BANKNIFTY)

---

# 2. Structure of the Pattern

The pattern contains four major components:

1. Left Shoulder
2. Head
3. Right Shoulder
4. Neckline

---

# Visual Structure

          Neckline
--------------------------------

        \        /
         \      /
          \    /
 Left      \  / Right
Shoulder    \/ Shoulder
             \
              \
               \
              Head

Eventually:
- Price breaks neckline upward
- Bullish reversal begins

---

# 3. Core Psychology

The Inverse Head and Shoulders represents:
- Seller exhaustion
- Loss of bearish momentum
- Buyers gradually gaining control

---

# Left Shoulder

Sellers push market lower.

Buyers react temporarily.

---

# Head

Sellers create another strong selloff.

BUT:
- Momentum weakens internally
- Smart money begins accumulation

---

# Right Shoulder

Sellers fail to create new lows.

This is critical.

It signals:
# Bearish weakness

Eventually:
- Buyers overpower sellers
- Neckline breaks upward
- Trend reversal begins

---

# 4. Why the Pattern Works

The pattern works because:
- Bears become exhausted
- Institutions accumulate positions quietly
- Selling pressure decreases gradually

The neckline becomes:
# Major breakout resistance

When neckline breaks:
- Short covering begins
- Momentum buyers enter
- Bullish expansion accelerates

---

# 5. Conditions for a Valid Inverse Head and Shoulders

# A. Prior Downtrend Required

The pattern must form after:
- Clear bearish trend

Without prior downtrend:
- Pattern loses reliability

---

# B. Clear Shoulders

Shoulders should:
- Be visually identifiable
- Have similar depth

Perfect symmetry NOT required.

---

# C. Head Must Be Lowest Point

Head should:
- Sweep liquidity below shoulders
- Create lower low

This often traps late sellers.

---

# D. Valid Neckline

Neckline connects:
- Swing highs between shoulders and head

Can be:
- Horizontal
- Slightly sloping

---

# E. Volume Behavior

Ideal behavior:

## Left Shoulder
Higher selling volume

## Head
Lower selling momentum

## Right Shoulder
Weak selling pressure

## Neckline Breakout
Strong bullish volume spike

# 8. Entry Strategies

# Strategy 1 — Neckline Breakout Entry

# Bullish Entry

Enter when:
- Candle closes above neckline

Requirements:
- Strong bullish candle
- Volume expansion
- Momentum confirmation

---

# Ideal Breakout Candle

- Large bullish body
- Strong close near highs
- Minimal upper wick

Avoid:
- Weak breakout candles
- Low volume breakouts

---

# Stop Loss

Place below:
- Right shoulder low
OR
- Neckline retest zone

---

# Target

Measured Move Technique:

Target =
Distance from head to neckline projected upward

---

# Example

Head:
21,700

Neckline:
22,000

Difference:
300 points

Breakout:
22,000

Target:
22,300

---

# Strategy 2 — Retest Entry (Professional Method)

Preferred by institutions and professionals.

---

# Steps

1. Neckline breaks upward
2. Price retests neckline
3. Neckline becomes support
4. Bullish confirmation candle forms

Enter after confirmation.

---

# Benefits

- Better RR
- Lower fake breakout probability
- Safer entry
